The multi-sensory method

Six inputs. One body that learns to rest.

Deep sleep is not built through one channel. Across the five evenings, each practice cues a different sense, chosen for its effect on the nervous system.

Sound

Slow voice notes for low-light bedrooms. Headphones optional. No music swell, nothing performative.

Light

A sunset cue, a 9:30 PM screen-dim ping, and warm light for the last 45 minutes.

Temperature

Warm feet, cool head. A thermal cue your body already knows, pulled from sleep medicine.

Breath

Four-count breath sequences that slow your nervous system in under two minutes.

Touch

A four-minute body scan that closes the day in the muscles, not just the mind.

Story

A one-line written prompt that gives the day an ending, so the night does not have to.

Honest answers

Unwynd, in plain words.

Unwynd is a six-day midlife reset for men aged 35 to 53 in Bengaluru: five evenings on WhatsApp covering sleep, stress, focus, energy and connection, then a three-hour peer circle in person on Saturday. It brings together Let's Unwynd circles and the SLEAP sleep reset.

Ask us on WhatsApp
Is this therapy or coaching?

No. It is non-clinical and peer-led. If you have clinical insomnia, sleep apnoea or another condition, Unwynd complements your treatment and does not replace it.

I'm not someone who opens up. Will this work for me?

The evenings are private. Saturday starts with doing something together, and you can pass on any question. Nobody is asked to share more than they want.

When do batches start?

Batch 1 starts at 8 PM on Sunday 1 November 2026, with its circle on Saturday 7 November. From Monday 9 November a new batch starts every Monday, each with its own Saturday circle, 9 AM to 12 PM.

Where in Bengaluru is the circle?

A quiet venue in central Bengaluru, chosen to be easy to reach from Indiranagar, Koramangala, HSR Layout, Whitefield, Hebbal and JP Nagar. We share the address when your seat is confirmed.

How much does it cost?

₹1,499 self-paced or ₹3,499 guided. You pay only after your seat is confirmed.

What if it does not work for me?

A full refund within 14 days of starting if it has not moved your sleep. No form. One WhatsApp message.

What if I miss an evening?

Pick it up the next day. The messages stay with you, so you can replay any evening. Your buddy will ask, kindly.

Is it confidential?

Yes. What is said in the circle stays there. No recordings, no notes, and phones go in a box for the three hours.

Do I need an app? What language is it in?

No app. Everything runs on WhatsApp, in English.

How do I join?

Message +91 98440 90505 on WhatsApp. Each circle has up to 12 men.
